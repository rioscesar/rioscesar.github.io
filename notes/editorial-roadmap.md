# Editorial roadmap

## Taxonomy

Use one primary category for most articles:

- `building` — product and project work
- `systems` — architecture, distributed systems, and infrastructure design
- `reliability` — observability, operations, incidents, and resilience
- `engineering-practice` — process, tooling, and engineering judgment
- `career` — leadership and professional growth
- `experiments` — prototypes, research, and ideas in motion

Tags are more specific and should aid retrieval, not become a second category system. Prefer a small, meaningful set such as `event-driven`, `windows`, `testing`, or `ai-assisted-development`.

## Editorial direction

Start articles with a real problem and a moment of tension. Show the original model, the evidence that challenged it, and the tradeoff behind the final direction. Link to repositories or artifacts when they clarify the story; do not add them merely as proof of activity.
